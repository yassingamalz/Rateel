## Notes for planning

- One book chapter may become **several app lessons** (e.g. ch. 10 «أحكام الميم والنون» covers
  noon/meem mushaddad, noon sākina and tanwīn, and meem sākina). Split it into sub-lessons here
  when planning, each with its own ✅.
- The **current app course list** (`src/assets/data/courses.json`, 12 courses) was written
  before choosing the book and does not match it one to one. The book is the reference: the
  course structure must be aligned to the book. Propose the new structure to the owner before
  changing it.
- Lesson 1 currently in the app corresponds to chapter 02 «علم التجويد» (definition, ruling,
  goal, types of laḥn). Chapter 01 «القرآن الكريم» may come before it as an introduction.

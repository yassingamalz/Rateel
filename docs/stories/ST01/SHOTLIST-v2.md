## Nonnegotiable framing

- **Do not title this “an eyewitness reconstruction of the first night.”** Revelation on Laylat al-Qadr is a religious reference (Q 97:1), while Bukhārī 3 does **not** name the specific hour of this cave encounter. Day / sunset / night transitions are cinematic devices.
- The Prophet ﷺ, Jibrīl and Khadīja are **not visually depicted in finalized revelation scenes**; anonymous travelers in early landscape references are **not** any of these figures.
- White-gold rays and floating letters are visual metaphors **not** source-backed visual phenomena. **No anthropomorphic depiction** of God or angels.
- Generated Arabic text, especially entire āyāt, **must not** be accepted as accurate; keep artwork text-free, add professionally typeset verified script in post-production.
- Use genuine licensed Qurʾān recitation; no synthesized Prophet voices and no AI-generated Qurʾānic recitation.
- If the text and actual recitation do not fit within 60 seconds, split the narrative or shorten the quotation rather than rushing.
- References do **not** override the primary sources. Historical / religious review required before publication.

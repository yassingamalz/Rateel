# ST01 — Sources, factual claims and editorial cautions

**Status:** preliminary source check (2026-11-23); **not** final religious or academic sign-off.

## Primary historical narration

- **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3**, *Kitāb Badʾ al-Waḥy*, narration from **ʿĀʾisha**: beginning of revelation; truthful dreams, seclusion at the cave of Ḥirāʾ, the encounter and command to read, first revealed verses, return to Khadīja and her reassurance, and meeting with Waraqa. [Arabic/English text](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3).
- **Qurʾān 96:1–5** (Sūrat al-ʿAlaq): opening verses traditionally connected with the first revelation. [Read with official orthography checks before typesetting](https://quran.com/96/1-5).

## Approval gates

- Verify any quoted Arabic against the source, with full tashkīl and correct Qurʾānic orthography.
- If recitation of Qurʾān is used, obtain licensed/permissioned genuine qāriʾ audio; do not synthesize Qurʾān or imitate a historical voice.
- Any retelling must avoid inventing details to fill time: no asserted weather, time of day, geography, exact outfits, positions of witnesses or visible heavenly phenomena without evidence.
- Representation practice differs across Muslim audiences. For maximum compatibility **do not depict the Prophet ﷺ or the angel Jibrīl**, even from behind; visualize the location, associated material culture and symbolic moods only.
- History/Tajwīd qualified reviewer to approve before publication.


## Additional historic beats for v2

[Sahih al-Bukhari 3](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3) (narration from ʿĀʾisha) explicitly covers the ordered beats: truthful dreams, retreat to the cave of Ḥirāʾ with provisions, the angel's command `اقْرَأْ` on three occasions accompanied by the pressings, transmission of opening verses from Sūrat al-ʿAlaq, the return in agitation, `زَمِّلُونِي زَمِّلُونِي` (cover me), Khadīja's reassurance, and her later taking him to Waraqa ibn Nawfal. These are **narrative facts to cite, not instructions to act out named sacred figures on screen**.

The revelation of the Qurʾān in **Laylat al-Qadr** is mentioned in [Qurʾān 97:1](https://quran.com/97/1), but the Bukhārī 3 account does **not give a verified exact hour of the cave encounter**. No literal story detail should be inferred from illustrations showing night, sun, lunar phase or luminous script.

### Distinction between texts and visual media

- In the current **SC04 beam-from-sky** reference, luminous script drifting downward is **not described in the hadith**. Label it artistic symbolism, not eyewitness description.
- Qurʾān 96:1–5 need to be copied directly from a verified muṣḥaf, with correct orthography, vowel signs and review; do **not** reuse AI-baked calligraphy in existing renders.
- Waraqa's conversation is source-backed but it is not to be compressed into a claim that everything happened during a single night. Reserve the topic for a distinct story or follow-up episode.
- Khadīja's words can be narrated authentically (following Bukhārī 3), while the first-version ST01 domestic art is **not** accepted as a literal representation of the couple.
- Future scripts must keep enough time for an actual human reciter; if accurate recitation exceeds the allotted window, shorten the scene's spoken part rather than speed up the Qurʾān.

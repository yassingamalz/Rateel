# ST01 — Sources, factual claims and editorial cautions

**Status:** preliminary source check (2026-11-23); **not** final religious or academic sign-off.

## Primary historical narration

- **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3**, *Kitāb Badʾ al-Waḥy*, narration from **ʿĀʾisha**: beginning of revelation; truthful dreams, seclusion at the cave of Ḥirāʾ, the encounter and command to read, first revealed verses, return to Khadīja and her reassurance, and meeting with Waraqa. [Arabic/English text](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3).
- **Qurʾān 96:1–5** (Sūrat al-ʿAlaq): opening verses traditionally connected with the first revelation. [Read with official orthography checks before typesetting](https://quran.com/96/1-5).

## Approval gates

- Verify any quoted Arabic against the source, with full tashkīl and correct Qurʾānic orthography.
- If recitation of Qurʾān is used, obtain licensed/permissioned genuine qāriʾ audio; do not synthesize Qurʾān or imitate a historical voice.
- Any retelling must avoid inventing details to fill time: no asserted weather, time of day, geography, exact outfits, positions of witnesses or visible heavenly phenomena without evidence.
- Representation practice differs across Muslim audiences. For maximum compatibility **do not depict the Prophet ﷺ or the angel Jibrīl**, even from behind; visualize the location, associated material culture and symbolic moods only.
- History/Tajwīd qualified reviewer to approve before publication.

# ST01 — Eight-shot prompt pack v2

**Status:** production-direction drafts, not final images or storyboard approval.
**Deliverable:** one still illustration per shot, **landscape 16:9**, free of AI-generated Arabic text and interface elements. Episode maximum **60 seconds**. Historical facts are grounded in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) and separated from visual metaphors in [SHOTLIST-v2.md](SHOTLIST-v2.md).

## Shared style for all shots

Original contemplative painterly stylized cinematic 3D inspired by the broad artistic feeling of a desert journey: vast sparse mountain landscapes, gentle hand-shaped geology, warm amber and terracotta rock, sage-green daylight sky, indigo-blue night sky, softened cloud shapes and mist, dramatic yet gentle natural light, restrained movement, deep silence, huge sense of scale, minimal modern detail. Historically evocative 7th-century Arabian material culture **only when supportable**, otherwise simple nondescript spaces. No direct replication of game-specific characters, costume silhouettes, scarves, logos or scenery. No anthropomorphic sacred light, no representations of Allah, Jibrīl or the Prophet ﷺ. No music cues, no text baked into art, no fantasy ruins, no accurate map claim, no modern objects.

Very wide golden dawn view of imposing rocky Arabian mountain landscape, distant rounded ochre valleys, haze over the ground, subtle green-gold sky and understated clouds. A tiny anonymous traveler may serve as scale marker **but must never be named or implied to be the Prophet ﷺ**. No sacred light: the sun is ordinary sunshine. Leave negative space for editorial title. **Existing art reference available.**

Austere empty cave of irregular warm brown rock looking out toward a vast valley at twilight, low orange sun and indigo dusk above distant mountains, airy dust, the dark rock gently illuminated by natural sidelight, no people and no props. This is an *interpretive stylization*, not claimed to be the actual cave photograph. **Existing art reference has a traveler; create people-free final variant.**

### SC04 (23–36s) — The first verses, exterior night

Wide distant view of a mountain under a deep indigo clear night sky, one narrow **single straight white-gold beam** coming down from high sky toward the mountain cleft; illuminate a small area only, keep foreground in calm shadow. Crescent moon optional as *nonfactual cinematic design*; no people and **no Arabic generated in the illustration**. The verses Qurʾān 96:1–5 may later be overlaid with a reviewed calligraphic font and authentic recitation. **Existing preferred mountain-only draft includes AI writing and is not publishable unchanged.**

### SC05 (36–42s) — Return along the mountain path, blue hour

**NEW REFERENCE REQUIRED.** Figure-free night exterior, descending narrow winding rocky trail from a mountain ridge toward a small distant settlement, moonlit blue-gray stone and soft warm amber lights far off, sparse sandy desert and faint haze. A subtle dynamic diagonal of the path suggests movement, but there are **no people**, horses, lanterns or invented narrative props. Deep quiet urgency without drama. Generic landscape only; the specific route and time are not documented.

### SC06 (42–49s) — “Zammilūnī”, warm interior

**NEW REFERENCE REQUIRED.** Wide quiet interior of a historically non-specific humble early-Arabian house, warm amber light through a narrow opening, simple woven textiles and a **folded plain covering/blanket** prominently in foreground, natural stone/plaster materials, minimal furnishings and a sense of safety. No people, hands or faces. Symbolic object shot supporting the historically narrated `زَمِّلُونِي زَمِّلُونِي` (Bukhārī 3); exact interior/objects are not documented.

### SC07 (49–55s) — Khadīja's reassurance, warmer interior

Same humble room as SC06, slightly wider composition, warm light softening shadows, folded textile gently illuminated; calm, dignity and reassurance suggested without depicting specific people or inventing physical actions. Night / amber interior is an artistic interpretation; only the conversation and reassurance are source-backed. **Previous two-figure concept is mood-only, not a final scene.**

### SC08 (55–60s) — New beginning / a new dawn

Wide dawn or early morning across an expansive sunlit valley and nondescript early Arabian settlement, sweeping sand dunes and soft clouds, horizon opened by warm golden sunlight; the symbolic forward journey leads into ST02. Can be entirely people-free; no depiction of a historical event, no identifiable buildings or scripture in the generated still. **Existing art reference available.**

## Shot-to-shot continuity

SC01 sunlight → SC02 warm twilight → SC03 deep night + restrained metaphorical light → SC04 wider night and one beam → SC05 blue hour → SC06 / SC07 warm shelter → SC08 sunrise. **This is an artistic day/night progression**, not a claim that Bukhārī's events occurred at those exact times.

The image generator must not render verses or the Prophet ﷺ as characters; any approved Qurʾānic typography goes into the video editing composition after independent proofreading.

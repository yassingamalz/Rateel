## Rules

- Never present any person in these images as the Prophet ﷺ, Khadīja, or a Companion.
- Generated Qurʾānic lettering **must be discarded**; overlay authorized verified Arabic typography in post-production.
- White-gold light is a creative metaphor; the hadith does **not** describe rays or text physically falling from the sky.
- Any precise time of day, settlement, mountains, moon phase, costume or residence is artistic and must not be narrated as historical fact.
- The next three visual references to generate are **SC03** (empty cave / restrained changing light), **SC05** (desert route at night, uninhabited), **SC06** (home interior / folded covering, no people).
- Do not re-use the game Journey's characters, scarf pattern, logos or other protectable details. The only borrowed quality is the general contemplative, painterly feeling of vast landscapes.

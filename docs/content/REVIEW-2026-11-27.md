# Content review, 2026-11-27 (Claude, at the owner's request)

Scope: everything on `content/curriculum-production-20261126` under `docs/content/` (batches 01–20, expansion 21–37,
missing-content 38–72, question-pool 73–98): about 1,270 assessment items, 650+ Quran examples, 65 narration drafts,
5 rule tables. Reading: Hafs 'an 'Asim, Shatibiyya path (the line of «التجويد المصور»).

## How it was checked
1. **Every Quran reference** (verseRef + excerpt) checked word by word against an Uthmani text
   (`quran-json` 3.1.2, KFGQPC-based text from quranenc.com), plus a second pass on the final vowel of each word.
2. **Every rule claim** read by hand: explanations, examples, questions with their keys and distractors, narrations,
   tables. Letter-rule consistency for nun/meem examples also checked automatically from the Uthmani marks.
3. **Structure**: JSON validity, answer keys inside the options, duplicate question ids: all clean (the only
   repeated ids are local option ids such as `1`, `a`, `s01`, which is fine).
4. **Coverage** of the 22 book chapters and of the sub-topics a Hafs course must have.

**Not done: page-by-page comparison with the book.** The 22 PDFs are on Google Drive; this session could not download
them (the Drive connector here is another account; the direct download was refused by the session's permission
filter). Every new or corrected item is marked `book_page_pending`. This check is in `docs/flutter/BACKLOG.md`.

## Verdict
- The doctrine is **sound**: no wrong rule was found in definitions, letter groups (izhar, idgham, ikhfa, iqlab,
  qamari/shamsi 14+14, qalqala, sifat, makharij 17), madd types, waqf marks, rawm/ishmam, meeting letters.
- **But it was not ready**: 17 Quran examples cited a verse that does not contain the word, 2 examples did not show
  the rule they were meant to teach, several "one of the known places" statements were wrong for Hafs, a few
  vocalisation slips, and important parts of the syllabus were missing.
- Fixed in this commit (list below). Everything stays `draft`/`blocked` until the book check and a qualified
  teacher's review (unchanged policy).
